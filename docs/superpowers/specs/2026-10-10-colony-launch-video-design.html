The look

  • Base: near black with a hint of violet, never flat black.
  • The swarm: hundreds of tiny ants, each the real Colony logo shape, moving like a flock of birds. Your brand guide planned an ant swarm and ant trails; this video is where they debut.
  • Words: one bold typeface (Inter, the brand font), very big, tight spacing. Each word arrives on the beat, with a quick blur that snaps sharp.
  • The app: the real Colony screens rebuilt sharp, floating like glass in 3D, slightly tilted, with soft shadows and light sweeping across.
  • Light and texture: colored glow around the swarm, soft depth blur behind, fine film grain.
  • Camera: always gently moving. Every change is one thing turning into the next.

Sound

  • Voice: a warm, confident narrator. I make 3 short samples from OpenRouter voices (ElevenLabs v4, Google Gemini 3.8, Microsoft MAI Voice 2.1) and you pick by ear.
  • Music: a custom track from Google's music AI (Lyria 3 Pro) through OpenRouter. It builds for 7 seconds, drops on "Introducing Colony", and resolves on the logo. If Google still blocks it, you pick a stock track instead.
  • Sound effects on every move: a rising whoosh as the words pile up, a soft rain of ticks for the swarm, a deep hit on the logo, clean clicks in the app, a ding on Approve.
  • Mix: the voice sits on top, the music dips under it, everything as loud as other videos on social apps.

How we build it

Four steps. You look after each one. Nothing moves on without your OK.

  1. Look proof. One finished still frame and 5 seconds of motion: the words breaking into the swarm and forming the logo. Silent.You check: does it look like a $10M launch? If not, we stop and rethink before building more.
  2. Voice and music tryout. Three voices reading the first line, and the music draft.You check: pick a voice and say yes or no to the music.
  3. Rough cut. The full 30 seconds with voice and music, every scene in place, motion not yet polished.You check: the story and the timing.
  4. Final. Polished motion, every sound effect, the mix, an MP4 for phones (1080 by 1920).You check: ready to post?

  • No black or empty frames. A frame brightness check runs on every render, because Promo1 once opened on black.
  • Words stay inside the phone safe area, away from the TikTok and Reels buttons at the bottom and right edge.

  • The stranger test and the banned word list on every line. No long dashes.

  • The logo keeps its true shape. No gradients or outlines on it.
  • The sound never clips.

For the builder

Technical notes
  • Code lives in video/: a standalone Remotion 4.0.522 package with its own lockfile. Never touch the workspace pnpm-lock.yaml.
  • New composition LaunchIntro: 1080 by 1920, 30 fps, 900 frames, in video/src/launch/. Add a render:launch script. Try 60 fps in the look proof; keep it only if it renders in reasonable time and looks clearly smoother.
  • All words, colors, voice lines and timings live in a job file, video/jobs/launch-intro.json, so a wording change needs no code.
  • Every frame must be a pure function of the frame number. Remotion renders frames in parallel and out of order, so the swarm cannot be a running simulation. Use seeded noise and closed-form paths: each ant interpolates between target point sets (glyph points of the words, river paths, logo points, card positions) with its own delay and a noise offset.
  • Target point sets: sample text glyphs from a canvas rasterisation of Inter, sample the logo from the brand mark geometry in desktop/src/shared/ui/colony-logo/ (docs/BRAND.md is the source of truth, 466:309 ratio).
  • Draw the swarm on a canvas each frame (a cached Path2D of the ant mark, rotated to its heading), not as hundreds of DOM nodes. Glow via a blurred copy of the same layer.
  • App screens are vector rebuilds of real desktop screens: "Your business" (desktop/src/features/onboarding/ui/new/screens/CompanyScreen.tsx), the team screen (pick from real screenshots), and the drafts with "Approve" (desktop/src/features/discovery/ui/CampaignOutreachTab.tsx). Check against just desktop-screenshot captures. 3D tilt with CSS perspective.
  • Voice via POST /api/v1/audio/speech on OpenRouter. Speech models list: GET /api/v1/models?output_modalities=speech. Music via google/lyria-3-pro-preview (chat completions, stream: true, modalities: ["audio"]).
  • Audio files go in video/public/launch/ with a SOURCES.md listing where each file came from and its license. Target loudness about -14 LUFS.
  • Brightness gate: ffmpeg frame sample scaled to 64 by 64 gray, mean above a threshold on every sampled frame except intended dark moments (the opening is meant to be near black, so gate on the glow area there).
  • Safe area: keep words roughly inside 90 to 940 px across and 150 to 1500 px down. Check against TikTok and Reels overlay templates before the final.
  • Promo1 code (grain, vignette, captions) exists only on this Mac, in the old spike worktree: 2 commits not on GitHub. Save them before reusing anything.
