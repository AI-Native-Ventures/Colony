Colony explainers, season 1

Design page, version 1. 11 October 2026. Follows the owner's review of 15 older Codex explainers (review page: https://claude.ai/artifact/NJHTfSJQ4hKHVYAb72GD7A) and the launch film he approved the same day.

What we are making and why

Eight short videos that each teach one thing about Colony, for people who have never heard of it: owners of small service businesses and people starting one. Each one stands on its own when posted, so each says what Colony is in one line before it teaches its idea.

The older explainers failed on craft, not topic: whole app windows shrunk until unreadable, a 3D mascot in a side column, "design concept" on every frame, slow openings, agent names nobody knows, five different looks. These videos use the world of the launch film instead.

Owner decisions

  • Wide 16:9 first (1920 by 1080, 30 fps). Tall 9:16 versions may follow later from the same scenes.
  • A different trade in each video, so each owner sees their own kind of business.
  • Pilot first: episode 5, "Makes your posts", for a bakery, built end to end and reviewed before the other seven.
  • Motion graphics only, drawn in code. No AI video, no mascot, no photos.
  • Voice: Gemini "Aoede". Definition line: AI employees are AI agents that do office work for you.
  • A customer's own work (posts, website, emails) is drawn in that trade's style, taken from Pinterest references for that trade.
  • Website in every end card: colony.global. No em dashes anywhere in the copy.

Every video, same shape

  • Hook, 0 to 3 s. A question or a pain the owner knows, in big words, voiced from the first second. Behind it, the ants swarm into that trade's tool (croissant, scissors, camera, spanner).
  • Meet them, 3 to 8 s. What Colony is in one line, then the employee: colour, badge, what it does.
  • The work, 8 to about 28 s. One card at a time, big and flat, with the work happening live: typing, ants carrying results in, a before and after.
  • Your yes, about 5 s. The owner approves. Nothing in Colony goes out without it.
  • End, about 4 s. Black ant logo, "Colony. AI employees for your business.", "Early access at colony.global".

Target 33 to 42 seconds. Rules carried from the launch film: never a whole app window; readable on a phone (body text at least 28 px on the 1920 frame); words on screen stay long enough to read; nothing new appears while the camera moves; no dark frames.

Look

  • The launch film's light world: soft pastel gradients sampled from the owner's posts, black Inter type, the black ant logo in the corner.
  • Employee colours as in the film: Scout violet, finds customers blue, sells amber, posts pink, website green. Small worker ants in that colour carry the work in.
  • The hook's ants swarm into the trade's tool, then scatter into the scene: the ants as pattern, never a character.
  • Colony's own screens are cards in Colony's style (Inter, white, rounded). The customer's work uses that trade's look from Pinterest, its own fonts and colours, so it reads as their business, not ours.
  • Captions light up word by word with the employee's colour on key words, as in the film.

Sound

  • One Aoede read per video through the Gemini API, read together so the lines flow, normalised to -16 LUFS and cut at measured pauses. Transcribed back to check every word.
  • Music: the launch film's draft B stand-in, re-cut per video, until real music credit is added.
  • Sound effects from the film: pops when cards land, a soft whoosh on moves, a click on Approve.

How we build it

  • A shared kit in video/src/explainers/: the five beats as a timeline, the shell (light world, corner logo, hook swarm, employee badge, approve beat, end card, captions, voice and music), and test helpers. Each video adds only its script, its timings and its work scene.
  • Reuse the launch film's parts from video/src/launch/wide/ rather than copy them: light world, worker ants, captions, Scout's plan, the website field and team, leads, email and call, posts, website repaint.
  • Each trade gets a small brand kit (name, colours, fonts, mark) in the style of its Pinterest references.
  • Compositions Explainer01 to Explainer08, 1920 by 1080, 30 fps.

Checks

  • Tests per video, written before the code: every line inside its beat, lines never overlap, the hook voiced within the first second, captions up long enough to read, the definition and banned-word rules, nothing appearing during a camera move.
  • After each render: loudness -16 LUFS, no dark frames, the same frame renders the same twice, the voice track transcribes in script order, a contact sheet looked at frame by frame.
  • Each finished video goes on one watch page and to the Desktop folder. One pull request into develop when all eight are done.
