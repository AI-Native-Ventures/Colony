Colony launch film, 90 seconds

Design page, version 2. 11 October 2026. Replaces the 30 second vertical plan after the owner's review of the rough cut.

What changed and why

  • Length: about 90 seconds, not 30. The owner: "30 seconds is too short, maybe a minute and a half."
  • Shape: wide 16:9 (1920 by 1080) for the site, YouTube, X and LinkedIn. Tall 15 to 30 second clips are cut from it afterwards.
  • App scenes rebuilt from scratch: the rough cut's tilted, shrunken app screens were "really really terrible". New rule, taken from the Codex-made Colony videos the owner liked (~/Downloads/Colony Intro 1.mp4, channels-desktop-v6.mp4): one idea per scene, one big flat element at a time, a big headline, lots of space, a calm camera.
  • Kept: the swarm opening (approved), the Aoede voice, motion graphics only, no AI video.

Two worlds

  • The dark ant world for the big moments: the opening, the switches between acts, the close.
  • The bright product world for the app: white and soft lavender, flat UI elements shown large, a big headline and a quiet subline, focus blur on whatever is not the point.
  • The ants are the employees. Each employee is a colored ant (the logo shape, with the brand walking gait). In the bright world the ants do the work on screen: they carry lead cards into a list like leaf-cutter ants, drop an email into the outbox, and so on. This is the film's signature.

Sound

  • Voice: Gemini "Aoede", new script read as one take and cut per line.
  • Music: a new 90 second track in the style of draft B (Seed Audio), with a quiet start, a lift on "Introducing Colony", a rhythmic middle, a build into "your yes", and a warm resolve.
  • Sound design on every ant action: soft ticks for footsteps, a pop when a card lands, a whoosh when work goes out.

How we build it

  1. Proof beat (next): 15 seconds of "meet the team: finds customers", wide, with voice, so the owner can judge the bright world and the working ants before anything else is built.
  2. The remaining three employee beats and "your yes".
  3. Opening and close in 16:9, "your day back", the 90 second music, the full voice.
  4. Polish, then tall cut-downs.

Checks

  • No placeholder art shown to the owner; only real rendered frames.
  • App text at least 20 px at 1920 wide; one focal element per moment.
  • Every screen element exists in the real app or is a plainly marked example; banned words never on screen (agent, workspace, model, channel, relay, token).
  • Voice transcribes to the script in order; loudness about -16 LUFS; no dark frames; same frame renders the same twice.
