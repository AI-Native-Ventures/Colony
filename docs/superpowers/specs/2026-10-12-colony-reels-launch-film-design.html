How it is built

Reuse, don't redraw

  • A new tall composition in video/src/launch/tall/ with the film's own timeline, voice, music and sound effects.
  • The film's cards and their ants are placed into the tall frame as they are, so every card looks exactly like the wide film's.
  • New pieces only where the shape changes: the employee row, the opening's positions, the day scene, the scroll between scenes, the captions.

Checks before you see it

  • The wide film stays byte for byte the same (its frames are compared before and after).
  • A test fails if any text box enters Instagram's covered areas.
  • Loudness, no dark frames, the same frame renders the same twice, the voice reads back in order, a contact sheet of every scene.

Order

  • Pilot first, 0:00 to 0:48 (opening, swarm, logo, definition, paste your website, Scout's plan). It settles the tall look before the rest is built.
  • Then the rest of the film, then the full 1:59 on the watch page and your Desktop.
  • Then the 8 explainers: they have the same shape (employee on top, card below), so they reuse this frame.
  • A 60 s cut can follow later from the same scenes if you want one.

Not decided here: music stays the stand-in track. Instagram's covered areas come from third-party guides, not from Meta; the pilot gets checked against a real upload on your phone if you want certainty.
